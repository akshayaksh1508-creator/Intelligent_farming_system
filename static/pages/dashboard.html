🏛️ Government Schemes for Farmers

Subsidies, income support, crop insurance and Kisan credit schemes.

INCOME SUPPORT

PM-KISAN

Direct income support of ₹6,000 per year paid in 3 installments of ₹2,000 every 4 months to farmers' bank accounts.

Eligibility: All small & marginal landholding farmer families.
CROP INSURANCE

PMFBY

Comprehensive crop insurance covering yield loss due to drought, floods, pests, and diseases at nominal premium.

Eligibility: All farmers growing notified crops in notified areas.
LOW INTEREST CREDIT

Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

Timely access to short-term credit for crop production, post-harvest expenses at subsidized 4% interest rate.

Eligibility: Farmers, tenant farmers, oral lessees, and SHGs.
SOIL HEALTH

Soil Health Card Scheme

Free soil test report issued every 3 years with nutrient status and crop-wise fertilizer recommendations.

Eligibility: All farmers across all states.
